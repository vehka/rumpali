# rumpali

Drum practice for norns. Rumpali plays a MIDI song, shows its drum part as
cues scrolling toward a hit line, and scores your pad hits from an
electronic kit or drum machine.

## Pages

E1 switches page.

**track**: play a song and drum along.

- K3: play / pause. Resuming rewinds one bar and counts you in.
- K2: stop and rewind (or show the song name when stopped).
- E2: next / previous song (when stopped). E3: tempo.
- Header: bar number, your last hit (`perfect +12`: 12 ms late), hits/misses.
- When the song ends: per-lane results with perfect / good / miss / extra
  and your mean offset in ms (positive = late). K3 plays it again.

**free**: no song, your hits scroll away from the hit line on the right.
K2 clears, E3 zooms.

**learn**: E2 picks a sound, hit a pad to assign it; it moves on to the next
sound. K3 twice clears the learned pads of the current kit profile.

## The screen

Five lanes, top to bottom like drum notation; track mode only shows the lanes
the song uses.

| lane | sounds | glyphs |
|---|---|---|
| CY | crash, ride, ride bell | ✳ star, • dot, ◇ diamond |
| HH | hat closed, open, pedal | x, o, low dash |
| SD | snare, side stick, clap | filled block, hollow box |
| TM | toms, other percussion | flat block, higher = higher tom; ◇ percussion |
| BD | kick | filled block |

Brightness is velocity: ghost notes are dim, accents bright. Your hits are
drawn as thin vertical marks at the time you played them, next to their cue.
Played cues dim, missed cues nearly vanish.

A big countdown (4 3 2 1) shows in the bar before the drums come in, and after
any rest of at least `countdown after` bars.

## Songs

`.mid` files in `dust/data/rumpali/songs/`. The drum part must be on
channel 10 with General MIDI drum notes; all other channels play as backing.
A demo song is put there when the folder is empty.

## Sound

Rumpali sends MIDI, to `sound out` (PARAMS > midi):

- the backing channels of the song, with velocities scaled by
  `backing volume` (PARAMS > song),
- the song's own drums, if `guide drums` is quiet or full,
- your hits as GM drum notes on channel 10, if `play my hits` is on
  (never back to the port they came from). `my hits velocity` scales their
  velocity (up to 400%, capped at 127) if your kit plays too quietly.

Recommended: [nb_fluid](https://github.com/vehka/nb_fluid). Enable the mod,
assign its `fluidsynth` device to a port in SYSTEM > DEVICES > MIDI, and pick
that port as `sound out`. Any GM sound module works too.

## Drums in

`drums in` is the MIDI port your kit is on (or all), `drum kit` the profile:

- **GM**: General MIDI drum notes on any channel.
- **Channels**: each track on its own channel (track 1 = channel 1 ...). Should
  work with most drum machines. (Tested on Analog Rytm.) Use learn mode to map the pads accurately.

## Timing

PARAMS > timing:

- `latency`: subtracted from your hits. If your results show you consistently
  late with a steady feel, play a song and use `latency += last mean`.
- `perfect window` (±35 ms) and `good window` (±90 ms), in real time, so they
  don't widen when the tempo is slowed.

`autoplay (test)` plays the drum part as fake hits, with some timing error
and misses, for trying it without a kit.

Settings are saved in `dust/data/rumpali/settings.pset` when the script stops.

## Testing without a kit

From maiden's REPL: `rumpali_hit(38)` hits a snare (`rumpali_hit(note, vel, ch)`).

Offline tests: `lua5.3 tests/run.lua` (optionally with a `.mid` path).
The demo song is generated by `lua5.3 tools/make_demo.lua`.
